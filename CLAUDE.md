## Usability standard

Read [the design standard](./DESIGN.md) before user-facing design or implementation.
Use its checklist and review record alongside these project instructions.
Preserve the user's mental model, make actions and outcomes clear, and explain why a result is right, wrong, or partially supported.
Check every affected surface and preserve the product's existing visual guidance.
